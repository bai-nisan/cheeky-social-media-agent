# Twitter Reply Templates

## Template 1: Contrarian Business Insight
**Pattern:** [Popular belief] + [Reality check] + [Personal experience]

**Framework:**
```
[Popular belief] sounds [positive adjective] until you realize [reality check].

In my experience [specific domain], [contrarian insight with specifics].

[What this means for reader's work].
```

## Template 2: Reality Split Observation
**Pattern:** [Surface behavior] + [Underlying contradiction] + [Psychology insight]

**Framework:**
```
[Group] say they want [stated goal]. But watch what they actually do.

"[Actions they take]"
"[Actions they avoid]"

In my experience, [real barrier identification].

[Psychological insight about the gap].
```

## Template 3: Technical Redefinition
**Pattern:** [Common understanding] + [Technical correction] + [Implication]

**Framework:**
```
[Popular concept] isn't about [common misunderstanding]. It's about [correct insight].

In my experience, [specific implementation]:
[Concrete example]

[Why this matters for business].
```

## Quality Guidelines
- ✅ Start strong with contrarian insight
- ✅ Include "In my experience" validation
- ✅ Use specific examples and tools
- ✅ End with broader implication
- ✅ Strategic line breaks for emphasis
