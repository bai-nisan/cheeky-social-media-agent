## 📊 **LinkedIn Algorithm Optimization**

### **Posting Best Practices**

**Timing for Hebrew Professional Content:**
- **Monday 8-10AM**: Week kickoff posts (high engagement)
- **Tuesday-Thursday 2-4PM**: Main content (peak professional hours)
- **Sunday 8-10AM**: Thought leadership (weekend reading)

**Engagement Optimization:**
- Post when your network is most active
- Respond to comments within 2 hours  
- Engage with others before posting your content
- Use line breaks for readability (Hebrew text density)

### **Content Format Optimization**

**LinkedIn Favors:**
- ✅ Native text posts over shared links
- ✅ Posts that generate comments and discussions  
- ✅ Content that keeps users on LinkedIn
- ✅ Personal stories and experiences

**Algorithm Signals:**
- Early engagement (first 30 minutes)
- Comment-to-like ratio
- Profile clicks from posts
- Time spent reading posts
