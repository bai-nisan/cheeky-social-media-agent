## 📊 **Analytics Capabilities**

### **LinkedIn Analytics (Core Features)**
- **`get_linkedin_posts()`** - Retrieve all LinkedIn posts with metrics
- **`get_analytics()`** - Deep analytics for LinkedIn performance  
- **`get_best_time_to_post()`** - Optimal posting times for LinkedIn
- **`get_metrics()`** - Available metrics for LinkedIn analysis

### **Future Expansion (Later)**
- **Twitter/X integration** - Can be added later for cross-platform insights
- **Multi-platform analytics** - Compare performance across platforms when ready

### **Content Management**
- **`post_schedule_post()`** - Schedule LinkedIn posts in advance
- **`get_scheduled_posts()`** - View your content calendar
- **`update_schedule_post()`** - Modify scheduled content
