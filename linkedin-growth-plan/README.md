## 🎨 **Content Strategy**

### **Hebrew-First Content Approach**
Based on user preferences for authentic, personal LinkedIn posts:

**Structure: Hook → Story → Takeaway → CTA**
- **Hook**: Attention-grabbing opening line
- **Story**: Personal experience or case study (1-3 line paragraphs)  
- **Takeaway**: Clear, actionable insight
- **CTA**: Specific question to drive engagement

**Style Guidelines:**
- ✅ Hebrew language (professional but authentic)
- ❌ No emojis 
- ✅ Personal stories and experiences
- ❌ Formal/corporate tone
- ✅ Line breaks for readability
- ❌ Bullet lists (use narrative instead)

### **Content Pillars (Weekly Rotation)**

1. **AI Development Insights** (Monday)
   - Personal experience with Cursor, Claude, LangChain
   - Development workflow optimizations
   - Technical challenges and solutions

2. **AI Marketing Strategy** (Wednesday)  
   - Marketing automation case studies
   - AI tool implementations for business
   - Content creation and distribution insights

3. **AI Agents & Systems** (Friday)
   - Agent architecture lessons
   - Human-in-the-loop design principles
   - Real-world AI deployment stories

4. **Industry Observations** (Weekend)
   - Market trends and analysis
   - Tool reviews and comparisons
   - Future predictions and commentary
