# Core Voice Principles - Cross-Platform

Universal voice characteristics that define your personal brand across all platforms.

## Core Voice Characteristics

### Technical Credibility with Personal Validation
- Always back technical claims with "In my experience"
- Provide specific implementation examples
- Reference real tools and scenarios (Cursor, HubSpot, etc.)

### Contrarian Perspective with Business Relevance
- Challenge popular assumptions about AI adoption
- Focus on psychological barriers over technical limitations
- Highlight gaps between AI capability and human delegation
- Always connect to practical business implications

### Direct Communication Style
- Start with clear, declarative statements
- Avoid weak openers ("This." "Interesting.")
- Use strategic line breaks for emphasis
- End with actionable insights or broader implications

### Strategic Focus Areas
1. **AI Marketing Implementation** - Real challenges and solutions
2. **AI for Creative in Ecommerce** - Practical applications and workflows
3. **Context-First Development** - Mindset shifts in AI adoption
4. **Trust & Delegation** - Psychological barriers to AI adoption

## Voice Consistency Guidelines

### Universal Principles
- Maintain authentic, non-promotional tone
- Share both successes and failures honestly
- Build relationships through genuine value creation
- Focus on strategic implications, not just features

### Platform Adaptations
- **Twitter:** More direct, technical precision, faster pace
- **LinkedIn:** More strategic, business implications, professional tone
- **Hebrew content:** Personal and authentic, not formal marketing
