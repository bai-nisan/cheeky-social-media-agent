## Content Opportunity Identification

### Hot Topic Indicators
✅ **High Potential Topics:**
- Multiple mentions across different sources within 48 hours
- Industry survey releases with surprising findings
- Major AI tool launches or updates affecting marketing/development
- Contrarian takes getting significant pushback (opportunity for nuanced perspective)

### Trending Topic Categories

#### 🔥 **Immediate Response (24-48 hours)**
- Breaking AI news with business implications
- Viral takes that miss key nuances (your contrarian angle opportunity)
- Industry survey results that warrant deeper analysis
- Tool launches that affect your audience

#### 📈 **Strategic Content (1-2 weeks)**
- Emerging patterns in AI adoption
- Deeper analysis of industry trends
- Synthesis of multiple related developments
- Original research or case study opportunities

#### 🎯 **Evergreen Angles (1+ months)**
- Fundamental shifts in how teams work with AI
- Long-term implications of current trends
- Strategic frameworks for AI adoption
- Educational content on complex topics

## Contrarian Angle Development

### Question Framework for Finding Unique Perspectives:
1. **What assumption does everyone make that might be wrong?**
2. **What practical implementation challenge are people ignoring?**
3. **What psychological barrier is more important than the technical one?**
4. **What business implication is everyone missing?**
5. **What does your experience tell you that contradicts popular wisdom?**

### Your Proven Contrarian Angles:
- Psychological barriers > Technical capabilities
- User learning > Raw features as competitive moats
- Context-first > Code-first development approaches
- Delegation trust > AI accuracy as adoption factor
- Transparency > Perfection for building AI trust
