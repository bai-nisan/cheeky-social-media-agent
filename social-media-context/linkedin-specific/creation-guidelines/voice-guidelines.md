# LinkedIn Voice Guidelines

Guidelines for maintaining consistent, effective voice on LinkedIn that aligns with your personal brand and resonates with Israeli professional audience.

## Core Voice Characteristics

### Hebrew Language & Authentic Tone
- **Primary language: Hebrew** - For Israeli professional audience connection
- **Personal and authentic** - Not formal corporate marketing tone
- **Professional but approachable** - Balance expertise with relatability
- **No emojis** - Clean, professional presentation

### Structured Communication
- **Hook → Story → Takeaway → CTA format** - Proven engagement structure
- **1-3 line paragraphs** - Use line breaks for readability and visual appeal
- **Single clear message** - Focus on one main point per post
- **Specific CTA questions** - End with engaging questions that invite discussion

### Content Philosophy
- **Strategic AI insights** - Focus on business implications, not just features
- **Personal experiences** - Share real implementation stories and learnings
- **Contrarian perspectives** - Challenge conventional thinking in AI/tech
- **Business relevance** - Always connect to practical business impact

## Successful Content Patterns

### 1. Personal Experience + Business Insight
```
Pattern: [Personal story/observation] + [Broader business implication] + [Engaging question]

Example approach: Share specific experience with AI implementation → Extract strategic insight → Ask how others approach similar challenges
```

### 2. Contrarian Industry Analysis
```
Pattern: [Common belief in industry] + [Alternative perspective] + [Supporting experience] + [Discussion starter]

Focus on: AI adoption barriers, development practices, marketing automation, technology decision-making
```

### 3. Strategic Technology Commentary
```
Pattern: [Technology trend/news] + [Strategic business angle] + [Personal validation] + [Forward-looking question]

Themes: AI marketing, development workflows, business automation, technology leadership
```

## Hebrew Voice Guidelines

### Tone Characteristics
- **אותנטי ואישי** (Authentic and personal) - Share real experiences and insights
- **מקצועי אך לא פורמלי** (Professional but not formal) - Maintain expertise without corporate stiffness
- **בעל דעה וביקורתי** (Opinionated and critical) - Don't shy away from contrarian views
- **מעשי ורלוונטי** (Practical and relevant) - Always tie to business implications

### Structural Elements
- **פתיח מושך** (Engaging hook) - Start with compelling observation or contrarian insight
- **סיפור אישי** (Personal story) - Include specific experience or implementation example
- **תובנה עסקית** (Business insight) - Extract broader strategic implication
- **שאלה לדיון** (Discussion question) - End with specific question that invites engagement

## Quality Checklist for LinkedIn Posts

Before posting in Hebrew, ensure:
- [ ] **ברור ומיושר למטרה** (Clear and mission-aligned) - Supports your AI marketing thought leadership goals
- [ ] **אותנטי ואישי** (Authentic and personal) - Shares real experience, not generic insights
- [ ] **בעל זווית ייחודית** (Unique angle) - Provides contrarian or differentiated perspective
- [ ] **רלוונטי לקהל המטרה** (Target audience relevant) - Addresses Israeli tech/marketing professionals
- [ ] **מובנה נכון** (Properly structured) - Follows Hook→Story→Takeaway→CTA format
- [ ] **מעודד דיון** (Encourages discussion) - Ends with engaging, specific question
- [ ] **פיסקות קצרות** (Short paragraphs) - 1-3 lines with line breaks for readability
- [ ] **ללא אמוג'י** (No emojis) - Maintains professional, clean presentation
- [ ] **תקין דקדוקית** (Grammatically correct) - Proper Hebrew grammar and professional language

- **הוסף ערך** (Add value) - Provide additional insights, not just agreement
- **שתף ניסיון** (Share experience) - Reference personal implementation examples
- **הרחב את הדיון** (Expand discussion) - Build on original point with new angles
- **שמור על אותנטיות** (Maintain authenticity) - Keep personal, professional tone
