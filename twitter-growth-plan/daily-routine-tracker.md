# Daily Twitter Growth Routine Tracker

## 50-Minute Daily Schedule (Mon-Fri)

### Time Block A: Feed Scan (5 minutes)
**Goal**: Check AI-Forward Marketers & Signal Boosters lists → like/RT relevant content

**Process**:
1. Open Twitter Lists view (not main timeline)
2. Scan "Signal Boosters" list for high-engagement posts
3. Quick like/RT 3-5 relevant posts
4. Screenshot any reply opportunities for Block B

**Success Metric**: 3-5 engagements completed

---

### Time Block B: First-5-Min Reply (10 minutes)  
**Goal**: Reply to Signal Booster posts within 5 minutes of publishing

**Process**:
1. Check notifications from Signal Boosters
2. Draft thoughtful reply (2-3 sentences max)
3. Add value, don't just agree
4. Post reply and monitor for responses

**Templates**:
- "This. Also seeing [specific example] in [context]. The key insight: [addition]"
- "Great point! In our experience with [specific case], [related insight]"
- "Exactly! This reminds me of [parallel example] where [result]"

**Success Metric**: 1-2 high-value replies posted

---

### Time Block C: Craft Daily Tweet (15 minutes)
**Goal**: Create value-driven tweet from idea vault

**Daily Themes**:
- **Monday**: AI tool insights  
- **Tuesday**: Marketing automation tips
- **Wednesday**: Hyper-personalization examples
- **Thursday**: Agentic AI predictions
- **Friday**: Week recap + community question

**Process**:
1. Check today's theme
2. Pull idea from vault or current trend
3. Draft tweet (220-240 characters)
4. Add 1 emoji, relevant hashtag
5. Schedule or post immediately

**Success Metric**: 1 original tweet published

---

### Time Block D: Publish & Engage (10 minutes)
**Goal**: Post tweet and reply to commenters

**Process**:
1. Publish daily tweet
2. Monitor for first responses (set 30-min alert)
3. Reply to every comment within 2 hours
4. Thank shares/retweets personally

**Reply Style**: Keep responses short, genuine, ask follow-up questions

**Success Metric**: 100% comment response rate

---

### Time Block E: Discovery Sprint (10 minutes)
**Goal**: Find and reply to small accounts for discovery

**Process**:
1. Search "AI marketing" or daily theme keyword
2. Filter for accounts <5k followers
3. Find thoughtful posts with <10 replies
4. Craft valuable reply that showcases expertise
5. Follow if account aligns with niche

**Success Metric**: 2-3 discovery replies to small accounts
