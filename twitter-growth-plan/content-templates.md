# Content Templates for AI Marketing Twitter

## Daily Tweet Templates

### 1. Tool Discovery Template
```
Just discovered [AI Tool Name] does [specific capability] 

This changes [marketing process] forever.

[One specific benefit/result]

Who else is using [tool category] to [achieve outcome]? 👇
```

**Example**:
```
Just discovered Claude can analyze entire marketing campaigns 

This changes content strategy forever.

Cut campaign planning from 2 days to 2 hours.

Who else is using AI for campaign analysis? 👇
```

```
→ [Supporting point 3]

```

```
✓ [Step 1]
✓ [Step 2] 
✓ [Step 3]
```

### Thread Template 2: Tutorial/How-To
```
1/ How to build an AI marketing teammate that actually understands your brand (not just your data):

A step-by-step breakdown: 🧵

2/ Step 1: Define your brand's decision-making patterns

Most AI tools know your demographics.
Few understand how you make strategic decisions.

Document:
• Why you chose certain campaigns
• How you prioritize channels
• What trade-offs you make

3/ Step 2: Train on outcome contexts, not just outcomes

Don't just feed it "this campaign worked"
Include:
• Market conditions when it worked
• Audience state and mindset
• Competitive landscape
• Resource constraints

4/ Step 3: Build feedback loops for continuous learning

Your AI teammate should get smarter after every:
→ Campaign launch
→ A/B test result  
→ Customer interaction
→ Market shift

5/ Step 4: Implement multi-modal understanding

Text, images, video, audio, behavioral data
Your AI needs to process all signals
like a human teammate would.

6/ The result:

Instead of "run more Facebook ads"
You get "launch video content on TikTok targeting late-stage buyers who engaged with competitor pricing pages this week"

7/ Next week I'll share the exact tech stack we're using to build this.

What's your biggest challenge with current marketing AI? Let me know below 👇
```
