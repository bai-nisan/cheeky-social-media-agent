### Tweet 2/8 - Context  
```
The problem with current "personalization":

❌ Segment-based (broad buckets)
❌ Rule-driven (if-then logic)  
❌ Backward-looking (historical data)
❌ Channel-specific (email vs web vs social)

Result: "Personal" messages that feel robotic and irrelevant.
```
