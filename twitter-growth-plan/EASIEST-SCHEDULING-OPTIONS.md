## 🛠️ **Super Simple Setup Guide (Buffer)**

### **Step 1: Sign Up (2 minutes)**
```
1. Go to buffer.com
2. Click "Get started now"
3. Enter email and create password
4. Verify email
```

### **Step 2: Connect Twitter (1 minute)**
```
1. Click "Connect a social account"
2. Select "X (Twitter)"
3. Authorize Buffer to access your account
4. Done!
```

### **Step 3: Schedule First Post (2 minutes)**
```
1. Click "Create post"
2. Write your tweet
3. Choose date/time
4. Click "Schedule"
5. Your post is scheduled!
```
