## Red Flag Indicators

**Engagement Quality Issues**
- [ ] Engagement rate drops below 1% for 3+ days
- [ ] Reply quality decreases (generic responses)
- [ ] Follower growth rate drops 50%+ week-over-week
- [ ] Increase in unfollow rate (>5% weekly)

**Content Quality Issues**  
- [ ] Recycling same content themes 3+ times
- [ ] No original insights for 5+ days
- [ ] Decreasing thread performance
- [ ] Running out of content ideas

**Community Issues**
- [ ] Negative sentiment in replies increases
- [ ] Fewer meaningful conversations
- [ ] Loss of Signal Booster engagement
- [ ] Reduced mention frequency
